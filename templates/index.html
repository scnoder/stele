Paste a YouTube or Instagram URL to get an AI-powered analysis

I started building Stele because I realized there wasn't an easy way to save and organize the valuable scholarship, internship, and career opportunities I discovered through short-form videos. Existing tools helped with bookmarking, but they often missed the key insights and resources shared within the content itself. Stele is designed to transform videos into an organized, searchable collection of actionable information, making it easier to revisit opportunities when they matter most. Inspired by the ancient stone monuments that preserved history, Stele aims to capture today's digital milestones so no valuable opportunity is ever lost.
